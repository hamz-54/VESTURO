Needs Spotify Premium and a free app at developer.spotify.com. Add as a Redirect URI, then paste the Client ID.

Start playing in the Spotify app first. Gestures then control that player.

Gestures
  • Point with your index finger only, then flick it up: volume up
  • Point, then flick it down: volume down
  • Open hand, swipe right: next song
  • Open hand, swipe left: previous song
  • Pinch thumb and index finger, hold, then drag left or right: rewind or fast-forward (release to jump)
  • Closed fist, hold still for a moment: play / pause
  • Both hands are tracked; use either one. Pause briefly between gestures
